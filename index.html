01 / The Story

Built in the late 1950s. Brought back in 2026.

Jack and Lorene Tarter opened the Skyliner in the late 1950s — a classic Route 66 motor court with all the modern comforts of the era: air conditioning, in-room phones, carpeted floors, televisions.

Then time did what time does. The property faded. The neon went dark.

In May 2025, Cody Paige and Charles Palmer bought the building and got to work. New electrical. New plumbing. New HVAC. New walls. Ten rooms rebuilt from the studs, with updated bathrooms, comfortable beds, mini-fridges, and contactless entry — your own door code, available 24 hours a day.

In 2026, Cody became the sole owner of the Skyliner Motel alongside his wife, Tera, continuing the restoration and welcoming travelers back to this classic Route 66 motor court.

The original turquoise-and-red neon sign was fully restored. It's lit again, watching over Main Street like it did the first time around.
